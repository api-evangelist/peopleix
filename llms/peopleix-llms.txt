## People Intelligence (the category)

People Intelligence is the practice of answering any workforce question by
combining the data in your HR systems (the what) with AI-led conversations
across your people (the why), in one continuous loop. Where people analytics
stops at the metrics, People Intelligence adds the reasons behind them, at scale
and in hours. peopleIX is the platform built for People Intelligence, and
/people-intelligence is the canonical page for the category.

People Intelligence vs. people analytics: people analytics reports the metrics
already in your systems (the what); People Intelligence adds the why, from AI
conversations across your people, and fuses both into one answer. Analytics is
one engine inside People Intelligence, not the whole of it.

## The two engines

People Intelligence runs on two engines:

People analytics: answering workforce questions from the data in your HR systems
(headcount, attrition, time-to-fill, engagement, pay). peopleIX puts an AI Data
Analyst on top of 100+ integrated systems: ask in plain language, get the report
or dashboard in seconds. Page: /people-analytics.

Conversation Intelligence: answering workforce questions by running AI-led
conversations across your employees at scale, adaptive interviews that probe and
cross-reference, around 3,000 words per person from a 30-minute conversation. It surfaces the reasons your
systems never capture (why people leave, where onboarding breaks). For the
workforce, not sales-call analysis. Page: /conversation-intelligence.

## How peopleIX compares

- vs. BI tools and people-analytics dashboards: dashboards stop where your data
  stops. peopleIX runs the analysis AND fills the gaps your systems never
  captured, by talking to your people.
- vs. Employee surveys: conversations average ~3,000 words per stakeholder from a 30-minute conversation
  vs. ~100 for a typical survey response, probing what people did not think
  to volunteer.
- vs. Traditional consulting: hours, not 4 to 6 months. A fraction of the cost.
  The customer keeps the knowledge layer instead of the consultant.
